Privacy

Private by design.

Talking about politics with friends shouldn't mean giving up your votes.

Party

Hidden until you vote.

You judge the bill, not the label. A lawmaker's party appears only after you've voted on one of their bills.

Groups & areas

Splits need 5 voters.

A Yea/Nay split stays hidden until at least 5 people vote, or 10 if everyone voted the same way. Area scores wait for 10 players. No one's vote can be guessed.

Friends

Sharing is mutual.

A friend sees your votes bill by bill only if you both turn sharing on. Otherwise they see agreement alone.

How we built it

The stack

A native iOS app and a small API with its own database. Lawmakers, districts and photos are stored on our server, so the app never calls outside services at play time.
