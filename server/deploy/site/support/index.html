Support

Effective October 26, 2026

Need help, found a bug, or want to tell us something? Open an issue at github.com/ybai08/docket/issues. We read every one.

Common questions

How do I report a player or a group?

Open their page in the app and tap Report. Pick a reason and, if you like, add a note. We review reports within 24 hours. You can also block them from the same place.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Profile → Privacy → Delete account. That removes your data and your sign-in account, and it can't be undone. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Who can see how I voted?

Only friends who also share their votes with you, and only if you turn sharing on. Everyone else sees totals at most, hidden until enough people have voted.

Where do the bills come from?

From official legislature records: Congress through Voteview and congress-legislators, state legislatures through Open States and their own websites, and Austin through the city's open data. Each bill links to its source. Docket isn't affiliated with any government.

My district or lawmakers look wrong.

Districts are matched from your ZIP code, and a ZIP code can span more than one district. Docket uses the district that covers most of it. Open an issue with your ZIP code and we'll take a look.

Docket is not affiliated with any government. Bills and votes come from official legislature records, Open States and Voteview.
